You've read `CLAUDE.md`. This session produces exactly one deliverable:
the **Creative Direction Document** for SOSRG as a whole (not yet a single
world). Do not write, generate, or modify any code in this session —
if you're tempted to scaffold a component "to illustrate the idea," don't;
describe it in words instead.

Structure the document as:

## 1. Core narrative
One paragraph: what journey does a first-time visitor take, start to
finish, across all seven worlds (Theatre, Cinema, Literature, Music,
Dance, Art & Design, Craft)? What connects them into one continuous world
rather than seven separate microsites?

## 2. Emotional arc, mapped to worlds
Using CLAUDE.md Section 3's emotional progression, assign which world(s)
carry which emotion, and why that world is positioned there in the
sequence (order matters — justify it).

## 3. Visual and tonal identity
Not colors and fonts yet — the *feeling*: handmade vs. digital, warm vs.
cold, theatrical vs. minimal. Reference 2-3 real places, films, or
experiences (not just adjectives) that anchor this feeling concretely.

## 4. World-to-world transitions
For each adjacent pair of worlds in the visitor's path, describe in one
sentence how the camera/scene physically carries the visitor from one to
the next without a hard cut (per CLAUDE.md Section 4).

## 5. Interaction philosophy
What should reward exploration site-wide, independent of any single
world's specific interactions? What's the one interaction pattern that
should feel consistent everywhere (e.g., "hovering always reveals sound
before it reveals information")?

## 6. Open questions for me
List anything you need a real reference for (a specific building, a
specific film's lighting, an exact color) rather than guessing — flag it
here instead of inventing a placeholder and moving on.
