What I would remove completely

The current homepage has several patterns that dilute the message:

❌ Generic SaaS feature cards

❌ Random cinematic AI artwork

❌ Blue gradients everywhere

❌ Abstract marketing copy ("Discover Your Cast", "Hire the Best Crew")

❌ Cards that don't explain relationships

❌ Decorative animations without narrative value

Every animation should answer one question:

"Does this help tell SosrG's story?"

If not, remove it.

The biggest strategic change I'd make

Today the homepage appears to present SosrG as a platform with features.

I would redesign it to present SosrG as a movement with infrastructure.

That's a subtle but powerful shift:

Not: "Here are our services."
Instead: "Here's why artists in Bihar have lacked a complete ecosystem—and here's how SosrG is building one."

The product pages (casting, artists, events, chat, registration) can still showcase functionality, but the homepage's job is to make visitors believe in the mission before they ever click deeper. That narrative will make the existing features feel like evidence of the vision, rather than a disconnected collection of tools.
